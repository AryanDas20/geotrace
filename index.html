COMMON QUESTIONS

Understand the boundaries

Helpful answers before you begin an investigation.

Does GeoTrace AI prove where a photograph was taken?＋

No. It provides possible leads based on metadata, OCR, and visual similarity. Every result requires independent human verification.

What if GPS metadata is missing?＋

The workflow can still use visible text and visual similarity, but the result may be less specific. Missing GPS should be recorded, not treated as evidence of tampering by itself.

Where do the reference locations come from?＋

Your organization must curate a reference dataset with permitted photographs, location labels, coordinates, provenance, and quality checks. The uploaded presentation describes an illustrative catalogue of around 200 landmarks for an MVP; it does not provide the dataset itself.

Can the AI change or delete investigation records?＋

Not by default. Hermes can propose supported actions, but live state changes must be explicitly confirmed, checked against the user's role, and recorded by trusted backend services.

Are demo results real?

No. DEMO-001 and its sample OCR phrase and candidate scores reproduce illustrative content from the presentation. They are fictional and do not come from the image you upload.
